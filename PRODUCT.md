# Franklin Massage Studio

> register: brand

## Product purpose

Marketing site for Franklin Massage Studio, a boutique massage and bodywork
studio in Zürich. The site sells stillness: it should make a visitor feel they
have already begun to exhale. Primary goals, in order: (1) convey a refined,
serene luxury that justifies premium pricing, (2) present the treatment menu
clearly, (3) drive bookings, (4) sell the studio's two handmade body scrubs.

## Users

Discerning Zürich professionals and visitors who treat bodywork as ritual, not
repair. They have been to Aman, Aesop, Six Senses. They read the menu like a
tasting menu. Mobile-first browsing, often in the evening, deciding where to
spend a rare slow hour.

## Brand & tone

- Natural luxury. Warm, earthy, handcrafted, calm. Editorial, never clinical.
- Voice: quiet, assured, sensory. Short sentences. No exclamation, no hype.
- Wordmark: FRANKLIN, with MASSAGE STUDIO set small beneath in tracked caps.
- Tagline territory: stillness, ritual, touch, warmth, hands.

## Anti-references (what to avoid)

- Clinical spa/medical wellness: cold white, teal, stock-smile imagery.
- Day-spa coupon energy. Loud CTAs, badges, "book now!!" urgency.
- SaaS card grids, hero-metric blocks, glassy panels, neon.
- The sage-green-and-white wellness reflex. We lean deep olive + warm cream,
  not pastel mint.

## Aesthetic references

Aman Spa, Aesop retail, Six Senses, Como Shambhala. Plus the warm, residential
Zürich-modern feel of alexlakezurich.com. Generous white space, large editorial
serif type, warm intimate photography (candlelight, oils, hands, linen, olive
branches, terracotta), no harsh light.

## Offering

Massage rituals (signature, deep tissue, aromatherapy, hot stone, prenatal,
couples) plus two handmade body scrubs sold on site:
- Citrus Glow Detox Scrub. Dead Sea salt, coconut, grapefruit. 100ml.
- Calm Ritual Soothing Scrub. Brown sugar, jojoba, lavender. 100ml.
Both handmade in Zürich.
